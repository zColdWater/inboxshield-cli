4 Key Findings From the Live Passive DNS Scan

1. Four Famous Brands Remain Spoofable (and 45% Lack p=reject)

4 of 60 (6.7%) still ship zero DMARC enforcement: Bolt.new (bolt.new: none), Gumroad (gumroad.com: none), Hugging Face (huggingface.co: none), v0 (Vercel) (v0.dev: missing).

Another 23 (38.3%) stop at p=quarantine rather than full p=reject (33/60, 55%).

2. SPF Recursion Cliff: 9 Leaders Sit at 8–10/10 Lookups

RFC 7208 caps SPF at 10 recursive DNS lookups — exceeding it causes instant PermError failures across Gmail and Microsoft 365.

posthog.com (10/10), vercel.com (10/10), datadoghq.com (9/10), airtable.com (8/10), mailgun.com (8/10), perplexity.ai (8/10) plus 3 more have 0–2 lookups left. Use our Free SPF Flattener to inspect nested include trees.

3. BIMI Verified Logos Reach 41.7%, But VMC Trails at 30%

25 of 60 brands publish a default._bimi record (including Cloudflare, Loops, Render, Netlify, Clerk, Linear), and 18 (30%) attach a Verified Mark Certificate (a=*.pem) required for Gmail blue-check logos.

Meanwhile 35 (58.3%) still show generic letter avatars in recipient inboxes.

4. 91.7% Omit MTA-STS Transport Security; AI Coding Trails Last

Only 5 of 60 (8.3%) deploy _mta-sts (cloudflare.com, loops.so, mixpanel.com, render.com, posthog.com) and 6 (10%) publish TLS-RPT.

By vertical, Cloud & DB (95/100) leads while AI Coding (83.4/100) ranks dead last.

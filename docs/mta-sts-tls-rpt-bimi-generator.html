    Frequently Asked Questions
    
      
    
        What DNS records do I need for MTA-STS and TLS-RPT?
        
    Publish three records: (1) an A/AAAA or CNAME record for mta-sts.yourdomain.com pointing to the HTTPS host serving /.well-known/mta-sts.txt, (2) a TXT record at _mta-sts.yourdomain.com containing v=STSv1; id=..., and (3) a TXT record at _smtp._tls.yourdomain.com containing v=TLSRPTv1; rua=mailto:... to receive daily JSON TLS failure reports.
    
      
    
      
    
        Why does mta-sts.txt need exact MX patterns instead of IP addresses?
        
    RFC 8461 verifies that the sending server's resolved MX hostname matches one of the mx: lines in mta-sts.txt (supporting single-label wildcards such as *.google.com or *.mail.protection.outlook.com) and that the TLS certificate matches that hostname and chains to a trusted CA.
    
      
    
      
    
        Do I need a paid VMC certificate for BIMI?
        
    Yahoo Mail and Fastmail accept self-asserted BIMI records (a=;) as long as your domain enforces DMARC p=quarantine or p=reject and uses a valid SVG Tiny-PS logo. Gmail and Apple Mail require a Verified Mark Certificate (VMC) or Common Mark Certificate (CMC) referenced in a=https://...pem.
